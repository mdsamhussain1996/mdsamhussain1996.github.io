Research

My research develops rigorous theoretical frameworks for the analysis and control of complex dynamical systems. Current focus areas include synchronization of stochastic systems and consensus of multi-agent networks, alongside established work in fractional-order systems and quaternion-valued neural networks.

Research Areas

Research Themes

🔬 Current Work
𝒲

Stochastic Synchronization

Analysis of synchronization in stochastic differential systems under Brownian motion and Lévy noise perturbations, linking Lyapunov methods with stochastic stability criteria for uncertain neural networks.

🔬 Current Work
𝒢

Consensus of Multi-Agent Systems

Designing distributed consensus protocols for heterogeneous multi-agent networks under switching graph topologies, communication time delays, and stochastic disturbances — leader-follower and leaderless scenarios.

∂

Fractional-Order Dynamics

Prabhakar, φ-Caputo, and Mittag-Leffler kernel frameworks for modelling systems with memory. Controllability and stability of multi-term impulsive fractional systems.

ℍ

Quaternion-Valued Neural Networks

Finite-time, projective, quasi-projective, and adaptive synchronization of fractional quaternion-valued neural networks using sliding mode control and Lyapunov methods.

⊛

Mathematical Control Theory

Controllability analysis of Prabhakar and Langevin dynamical systems with non-instantaneous impulses. Extensions to multi-agent and switched system architectures.

𝛹

Nonlinear Dynamical Systems

Stability and qualitative analysis of nonlinear ODEs and functional differential equations, including competitive, inertial, and recurrent neural architectures with fractional-order dynamics.
