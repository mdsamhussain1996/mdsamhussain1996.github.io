Open Educational Initiative

📐 Maths for All

Maths for All is an open, freely accessible educational platform I created and maintain, aimed at making rigorous mathematics approachable for everyone — from school students and engineering undergraduates to working professionals. The platform emphasises intuition-first explanations, rich visualisations, and real-world applications, without sacrificing mathematical rigour.
