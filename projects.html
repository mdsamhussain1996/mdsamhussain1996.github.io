Distributed Consensus Protocol Simulator

Simulation of average-consensus and leader-follower protocols over switching graph topologies (ring, complete, star, random). Demonstrates convergence under communication delays and stochastic perturbations. The protocol implements $\dot{x}_i = -\sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}_i}(x_i - x_j)$ with discrete-time updates.

📊 Key Result: Convergence time scales with the algebraic connectivity $\lambda_2(\mathcal{L})$ of the graph Laplacian — sparser graphs converge slower.

Stochastic Synchronization under Brownian Noise

Numerical simulation of two coupled stochastic differential systems: $dx_i = f(x_i)\,dt + \sigma\,dW_t$. Tracks the synchronization error $e(t) = x_1(t) - x_2(t)$ and validates Lyapunov-based stability criteria under varying noise intensities $\sigma$. Implements Euler–Maruyama and Milstein schemes.

📊 Key Result: Mean-square synchronization is achievable even under noise intensity $\sigma \leq 0.3$ with appropriate coupling strength $c > \lambda_{\max}$.

Fractional-Order Quaternion Neural Network Synchronization

MATLAB simulation of finite-time synchronization of two coupled fractional-order quaternion-valued neural networks via sliding mode control. Implements the Adams–Bashforth–Moulton predictor-corrector method for fractional integration, tracking all four quaternion components $(q_0, q_1, q_2, q_3)$.

📊 Key Result: Finite-time convergence proven: $T^* \leq T_{\max}$ regardless of initial conditions, with Lyapunov function $V(t) \leq V(0) e^{-\beta t^\alpha}$.

Lyapunov Stability Region & Phase Portrait Explorer

Interactive phase portrait generator for 2D nonlinear dynamical systems. Plots level sets of Lyapunov candidate functions $V(\mathbf{x})$ alongside system trajectories, visually confirming that $\dot{V} \leq -\alpha V$ (exponential stability) or $\dot{V} \leq 0$ (Lyapunov stability). Used as a teaching tool for MATH courses at Newton School of Technology.

📊 Used in: Engineering Mathematics (Newton School of Technology) and shared on the Maths for All platform.
