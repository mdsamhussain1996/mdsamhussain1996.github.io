Mathematician & Dynamical Systems Researcher

I am a mathematician and researcher specialising in synchronization of stochastic dynamical systems, consensus of multi-agent systems, fractional-order systems, and quaternion-valued neural networks. I completed my PhD at IIT Mandi, focusing on synchronization of fractional-order quaternion-valued neural networks, and currently serve as an Assistant Professor at Newton School of Technology, Pune.
