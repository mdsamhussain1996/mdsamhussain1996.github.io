Mathematician & Dynamical Systems Researcher

I study synchronization and control of complex networks using fractional calculus, quaternion algebra, and stochastic analysis. My PhD thesis (IIT Mandi, 2021–2025) developed the theory of synchronization for fractional-order quaternion-valued neural networks, establishing Lyapunov-based stability criteria, fixed-time controllers, and applications to secure image encryption.

Currently Working On

Stochastic Fractional-Order Neural Networks — Extending synchronization theory to quaternion-valued networks driven by Brownian motion and Lévy jump noise.
LLM-Negotiation Swarms — Modelling multi-agent language-model consensus as a nonlinear fractional-order dynamical system and studying its controllability.
Event-Triggered Synchronization — Designing aperiodic control laws that achieve fixed-time synchronization with minimal communication overhead.
